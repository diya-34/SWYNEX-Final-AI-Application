⚠️ Transparent Limitations & Failure Modes

Academic AI systems must be honest about where errors can occur. Here are the documented edge cases and known limitations:

🔄

1. Multi-Intent / Mixed Announcements

When an announcement combines disparate topics (e.g., "Pay your hostel mess fee before semester exams begin"), the model selects only the primary statistical topic. Secondary intentions are reflected in the probability breakdown, but single-label classification cannot assign dual primary categories.

📅

2. Complex or Colloquial Relative Dates

Expressions like "the third Tuesday following the Diwali vacation" or "in a fortnight" cannot be resolved without a full campus calendar parser. The extractor marks these as undetected (None) and triggers a missing-date alert.

🚨

3. Misleading Urgency Keywords

Notices containing the word "urgent" in benign contexts (e.g., "urgent care clinic" or "urgent notice: lawn mowing in front of library") are addressed through contextual regex filtering, but complex sarcastic or figurative urgency may still raise priority.

✂️

4. Extractive Summarization Constraints

Extractive summarization strictly selects original sentences to eliminate hallucination. However, if the source circular is drafted as disjointed fragments, the summary retains that disjointed syntax rather than rewriting it gracefully.

🛡️ Ethics, Student Privacy & Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

🔒 Student Privacy Protection

  • Zero PII Collection: Students must never input sensitive personally identifiable information (e.g., roll numbers, passwords, marks cards, or medical certificates).
  • No Server-Side Storage: Announcements are analyzed in-memory and not logged or uploaded to cloud databases.
  • 100% Offline / Local: No data ever leaves your computer.

⚖️ Bias & Fairness Mitigation

  • Informal vs Formal Notices: Circulars from student clubs often use informal phrasing, while registrar notices use strict administrative language.
  • Hybrid Fallback: Pairing TF-IDF with keyword heuristics prevents rare student announcements from being rejected.
  • Auditable Evidence: Every prediction displays the exact keywords and confidence score.

👨‍🏫 Official Verification Requirement

  • Decision Support Only: This AI system is an informational assistant, not an official academic authority.
  • Mandatory Verification: For high-stakes decisions (e.g., exam registration deadlines or placement drives), students must verify with official college circulars.
  • Precedence Rule: Official notifications from the Controller of Examinations always take legal precedence over AI outputs.
